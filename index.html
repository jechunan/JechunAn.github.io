Hi! I am a Postdoctoral Assoicate at the University of Minnesota, Twin-Cities. Before joining academia in the United States, I began my career as a tenured elementary school teacher in a rural area of South Korea. I also earned an M.A. from Seoul National University. Until 2024, I served as the lab manager for the federally funded Early Writing Project, which provided professional development for elementary teachers supporting students with writing difficulties. I earned my Ph.D. from University of Minnesota Twin-Cities under the supervision of Dr. Kristen McMaster.

Research

My research focuses on integrating literacy assessment into data-informed instructional systems to support teachers working with students who experience significant difficulties in literacy (reading and writing) and language. Ultimately, My research goal is to develop equitable and instructionally useful approaches that improve literacy outcomes for diverse learners. My recent works explore:

  • Instructionally Useful Assessment: An (2024)
  • Personalized Learning Paths: An et al. (2026)
  • Coaching Support for Teachers: An et al. (2025), An & McMaster (2026)
